Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis – Final Report

Google Data Analytics Capstone | 12-Month Dataset | 5,475,207 Trips

Phase 1: Ask

Business Task: Determine how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently to inform a targeted marketing strategy that converts casual riders into annual members, maximizing long-term revenue growth.

Phase 2: Prepare

Loaded 12 months of Divvy trip data (May 2025 – Apr 2026). Derived columns: ride_length_minutes, day_of_week, hour_of_day, month, season, is_weekend. Validated schema and data types across all files.

Phase 3: Process

Cleaned dataset: removed duplicates, zero/negative-length rides, and applied station name normalization. Computed Haversine distances and round-trip flags. Final dataset: 5,475,207 rows.

Phase 4: Analyze

Descriptive statistics, pivot tables, and statistical tests:

  • Member avg ride: 12.54 min | Casual avg ride: 22.94 min
  • Ride duration t-test: t = -209.96, p_value < 0.001, Cohen's d = -0.19
  • Day-of-week chi-square: χ² = 144,895.1, p_value < 0.001, df = 6

Phase 5: Share

Six 300-DPI visualizations generated (ride duration, day-of-week, hourly patterns, seasonal trends, bike type, geographic heatmap). 10-slide HTML presentation produced for stakeholder delivery.

Phase 6: Act

Key Findings

  • Casual riders (35.5% of 5,475,207 total trips) ride 83% longer on average (22.9 min) than members (12.5 min), indicating leisure-driven usage patterns.
  • Members are predominantly weekday commuters (77% weekday trips) while casuals skew toward weekends (37% weekend trips), revealing distinct use-case segmentation.
  • Casual riders take 2.7x more round trips than members, confirming recreational rather than point-to-point commuting behavior.
  • Casual ridership peaks sharply in Summer and at tourist-heavy stations (e.g., Navy Pier), creating a high-intent seasonal conversion window.
  • Both groups show strong electric bike preference (casual 66.6%, member 64.7%), making e-bike access a viable membership incentive lever.

Top 3 Recommendations

  • REC-01 – Weekend Leisure-to-Membership Conversion Campaign: Launch a 'Weekend Warrior' membership tier priced between single-ride and annual plans, promoted at high-traffic leisure stations on Saturdays and Sundays via in-app banners and dock-side QR codes.
  • REC-02 – Summer Peak Season Membership Drive: Run a time-limited 'Summer Membership' promotion from June through August at top stations including Navy Pier, offering a discounted first-year annual membership with a free month for sign-ups during peak season.
  • REC-03 – Electric Bike Upgrade Incentive for Casual Riders: Offer casual riders who have taken 3+ electric bike rides in a month a targeted 'E-Bike Member' upgrade offer granting priority e-bike access and a reduced annual membership rate, delivered via in-app message.
