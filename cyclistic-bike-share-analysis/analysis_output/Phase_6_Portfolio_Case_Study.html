Cyclistic Bike-Share: Casual-to-Member Conversion Strategy

Google Data Analytics Capstone – Portfolio Case Study

Business Task

Determine how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently to inform a targeted marketing strategy that converts casual riders into annual members, maximizing long-term revenue growth.

Data Overview

Analysis of 5,475,207 trips across 12 months (May 2025 – Apr 2026), covering 1,809 unique stations in Chicago.

Key Findings

  • Casual riders (35.5% of 5,475,207 total trips) ride 83% longer on average (22.9 min) than members (12.5 min), indicating leisure-driven usage patterns.
  • Members are predominantly weekday commuters (77% weekday trips) while casuals skew toward weekends (37% weekend trips), revealing distinct use-case segmentation.
  • Casual riders take 2.7x more round trips than members, confirming recreational rather than point-to-point commuting behavior.
  • Casual ridership peaks sharply in Summer and at tourist-heavy stations (e.g., Navy Pier), creating a high-intent seasonal conversion window.
  • Both groups show strong electric bike preference (casual 66.6%, member 64.7%), making e-bike access a viable membership incentive lever.

Top 3 Recommendations

REC-01: Weekend Leisure-to-Membership Conversion Campaign

Insight: Casual riders average 22.9 min per ride vs 12.5 min for members, and take 2.7x more round trips (round-trip ratio 8.4%), indicating leisure use concentrated on weekends.

Action: Launch a 'Weekend Warrior' membership tier priced between single-ride and annual plans, promoted at high-traffic leisure stations on Saturdays and Sundays via in-app banners and dock-side QR codes.

Target Audience: Casual weekend riders

Channels: in-app notifications, dock-side signage, social media

Expected Impact: 10–15% conversion of frequent casual weekend riders

KPI Target: 500 new weekend memberships in Q1 campaign

REC-02: Summer Peak Season Membership Drive

Insight: Casual ridership peaks sharply in Summer (911,195 trips), representing the highest casual volume of any season — nearly 3x Winter casual volume. This seasonal concentration is a high-intent conversion window.

Action: Run a time-limited 'Summer Membership' promotion from June through August at top stations including Navy Pier, offering a discounted first-year annual membership with a free month for sign-ups during peak season.

Target Audience: High-frequency summer casual riders

Channels: email retargeting, push notifications, partner tourism apps

Expected Impact: 15–20% uplift in annual membership sign-ups during summer

KPI Target: 20% year-over-year increase in summer membership conversions

REC-03: Electric Bike Upgrade Incentive for Casual Riders

Insight: Casual riders already choose electric bikes 66.6% of the time vs 64.7% for members, showing strong e-bike preference. Members who use e-bikes ride more frequently on weekdays, suggesting e-bike access drives habitual use.

Action: Offer casual riders who have taken 3+ electric bike rides in a month a targeted 'E-Bike Member' upgrade offer granting priority e-bike access and a reduced annual membership rate, delivered via in-app message.

Target Audience: Casual riders with repeated electric bike usage

Channels: in-app messaging, email, loyalty program notifications

Expected Impact: Convert high-engagement casual e-bike users to annual members

KPI Target: 8% conversion rate among targeted casual e-bike users within 90 days

Expected ROI

Implementing all three recommendations is projected to deliver a 15% increase in annual membership revenue.
