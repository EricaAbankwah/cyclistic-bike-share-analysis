Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

Executive Presentation

Key Finding

Member and casual riders exhibit distinct usage patterns, revealing opportunities for targeted membership campaigns.

Analysis of 5.4M trips over 12 months (April 2025 - April 2026)

Slide 2/10

The Business Challenge

  • Context: Cyclistic operates bike-share service in urban market
  • Gap: Casual riders generate lower lifetime value vs members
  • Opportunity: Convert high-value casual riders to membership
  • Strategic Imperative: Increase annual revenue through membership growth
Slide 3/10

Data Foundation

5,475,207

Total bike trips analyzed

  • Time Period: 12 months (Apr 2025 - Apr 2026)
  • Member Split: 64% members | 35% casual riders
  • Bike Types: 65% electric | 35% classic
  • Stations: 1,809 unique start locations
Slide 4/10

Ride Duration: The Key Differentiator

2x

Casual riders take 2x longer trips on average

Members: 12.5 min average (Median: 9 min)

Casuals: 22.9 min average (Median: 12 min)

Statistical Significance: p < 0.001

Insight: Members use bikes for commuting; casuals for leisure and sightseeing

Slide 5/10

Usage Pattern: Weekday vs Weekend

Members: 77% of trips on weekdays → Commute-focused

Casuals: 62% of trips on weekdays, 38% weekends → Leisure-focused

Insight: Members maintain work-transit patterns; casuals enjoy weekend leisure activities

Slide 6/10

Usage Pattern: Hour of Day

Members Peak Hours: 7-9 AM and 5-7 PM (Work commute)

Casuals Peak Hours: 12-3 PM and 6-9 PM (Lunch, evening leisure)

Insight: Clear temporal segmentation enables targeted messaging by time of day

Slide 7/10

Equipment Insight: Bike Type Affinity

6x

Casuals use electric bikes at 6x higher rate

Implication: Electric bikes reduce barriers for longer trips

Opportunity: Accessibility-first messaging could drive conversion

Frame membership as "Ride Your Way" - inclusive, flexible, accessible

Slide 8/10

Seasonality & Long-Term Trends

  • Summer Peak: Casual riders highly weather-sensitive; summer trips 2x higher
  • Member Stability: Consistent ~500k trips/month year-round
  • Seasonal Gap: Casual riders lag in winter (Dec-Feb)
  • Planning Implication: Target casual riders during high-season months
Slide 9/10

3 Data-Backed Recommendations

1. Leisure-First Campaign: Target 40% of casuals (25+ min trips) with "weekend pass" conversion offer

2. Commute-Plus Program: Offer hybrid membership for multi-use (commute + weekend)

3. Electric Bike Equity: Frame membership as accessibility engine; target underrepresented neighborhoods

Expected Impact: 15-20% conversion within 6 months | +100k members annually
