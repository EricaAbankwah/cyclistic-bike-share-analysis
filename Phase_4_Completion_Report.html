🔑 Critical Insights

1. Usage Behavior Differs Significantly: Casual riders take significantly longer trips (83% longer mean duration) with higher variability, suggesting leisure-oriented usage vs. members' commute-oriented patterns.

2. Seasonal Patterns: Summer shows peak usage for both groups, with casual riders having more pronounced seasonal variation (911K summer vs 89K winter trips).

3. Peak Hours Convergence: Both groups peak during evening hours (5-6 PM), but casual riders extend further into later hours for recreational use.

4. Bike Type Consistency: Electric bikes dominate for both member types (65%+), suggesting preference for power-assisted commutes.
